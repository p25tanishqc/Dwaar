    How it works

    Set it up once. After that, you only hear from Dwaar when it needs you.

    Once

    Tell Dwaar who can receive what

    Your family is already in your flat's MyGate profile. Set limits once: Mummy up to ₹5,000, the help groceries only. Block ₹10,000 with UPI Reserve Pay for COD.

    Every delivery

    Dwaar handles the day

    It sees who is inside the society, calls the right person in their language, and agrees a time with the courier. No calls to you, no codes to anyone.

    Only when needed

    One tap from you

    A ₹18,999 phone above Mummy's limit? You get one push with three choices. Everything else ends as one record you can check anytime.

    Concept screens

    Three moments. None of them is an OTP.

    Dwaar speaks and listens in Hindi with Gnani's voice models, tested live in our simulation.

    Safety

    What Dwaar will never do

    • ✓
      Never ask anyone for an OTPNot on a call, not on WhatsApp, not at the door. If someone asks your family for a code "from Dwaar", it isn't us.
    • ✓
      Never pay before the rider is verifiedMoney moves only after the rider's phone matches your home. If the check fails, nothing is paid and you are told once.
    • ✓
      Never go above your limitsEach person's limit is yours to set. Anything above it waits for your tap, and the COD reserve itself is capped at ₹10,000.
    • ✓
      Never use your family's data for adsWho is home and when stays inside Dwaar. You can see it, revoke anyone and delete it anytime.
    Does it cost anything?

    No. Dwaar is free for households. Sellers pay a ₹10 fee, through the courier, each time Dwaar gets a cash-on-delivery parcel delivered on the first try. One failed delivery costs them far more.

    Which deliveries does the pilot cover?

    Delhivery parcels to pilot societies in Noida and Greater Noida first, including cash-on-delivery orders and return pickups.

    What if nobody I trust is home?

    Dwaar asks you once, with at most three choices: this evening, tomorrow morning, or leave it with the guard if it is prepaid. It never sends reminders.

    How does the COD money work?

    You block up to ₹10,000 once with UPI Reserve Pay in your own UPI app. Dwaar pays a COD amount from it only after the rider is verified. Anything bigger is a one-time UPI payment you approve. Unused money is released when the block ends.
